# Financial Information

Apps can exist in 5 states which are based on financial performance or secondarily VP discretion.

Initial State- Where the app starts
Growth State- After first time reaching $7.5K-$10K annual pace for 2 consecutive quarters or after reaching 50-100% pace of next “Income Tier” for 2 consecutive quarters. Up to 50% of net profit is expected to be reinvested into the app or ecosystem on a quarterly to yearly basis. These reinvestments will aim to bring the app through each of the income tiers
Maintenance State - This has two conditions. First, after an app has reached $5K annual pace for 2 consecutive quarters but has failed to reach the $7.5K pace for 4 consecutive quarters since reaching the $5K pace for 2 consecutive quarters status. Secondly, after an app has reached the growth state and has failed to reach 50% pace of the next Income Tier for 4 consecutive quarters since reaching its current income tier. In this state, only up to 10% of net profit is expected to be reinvested into the app or ecosystem on a quarterly to yearly basis.These reinvestments will aim to keep the app and ecosystem updated so that current customer and revenue levels are maintained. Otherwise, up to 50% can still be optionally reinvested if the decision is approved by the VP. However, this is not the default choice.
Regression State - This occurs when an app regresses to a previous income tier for 4 consecutive quarters. Money is reinvested the same as in the Maintenance state.This state classification is just for awareness of the app’s decline.
Divest State - This has three conditions. First, when the app regresses to break even or net loss for 2 consecutive quarters once previously in Maintenance or Regression state. Secondly, if the app never reached the $5K annual pace for 2 consecutive quarters since launch. Thirdly, the VP chooses to decommission or remove the app. In this state,  the platform prepares decommissioning of the app

There are 8 annual income tiers which an app based on 2 consecutive quarters of performance can reach.

Tier 1 - $7.5-$10K
Tier 2 - $18.75-$25K
Tier 3 - $37.5-$50K
Tier 4 - $75-$100K
Tier 5 - $187.5-$250K
Tier 6 - $375-$500K
Tier 7 - $562-$750K
Tier 8 - $750K-$1M+

The platform’s goal is to use it’s proprietary process to grow each app through the full range of Income Tiers quickly and reliably. The platform can expect to reinvest up to 50% of net profit to achieve the next income tier.

The platform must present in the report summary the start up costs estimates. Apps are expected to begin with $100-$5,000 in start up costs. If an app cannot fit within this startup requirement, then it is not suggested. The startup cost as it pertains to this determination will not include initial R&D for the build, testing and deployment. However those costs will be tracked in the profit and loss statements as expenses. For that reason, it is still ideal to keep those costs at a minimum without sacrificing effectiveness. 

All financial data per app and per ecosystem component will be made available to the platform either directly or via local documentation. The platform can use this data to enhance its predictions, tracking, analysis, etc.

Objective Level 1 - One app currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross $7.5-$10K
Objective Level 2  - One app currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross $18.75-$25K
Objective Level 3  - One app currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross $37.5-$50K
Objective Level 4  - One app currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross $75-$100K
Objective Level 5  - One app currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross 187.5-250K
Objective Level 6  - One app currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross 375-500K
Objective Level 7  - One app currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross 562-750K
Objective Level 8  - One app currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross 750K-1M
Objective Level 9  - Three apps currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross 750K-1M
Objective Level 10  - Five apps currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross 750K-1M
Objective Level 11  - Ten apps currently on pace over 2 consecutive quarters to annually gross 750K-1M
Objective Level 12 - All apps combined gross $30-35M
Objective Level 13 - All apps combined gross $50M+

Note: The objective levels only count for apps actively in the space. If any app is moved from the space for any reason, then it is no longer counted.


Quick notes on financial targets and other info. 

The platform can expect for the after tax (California) net profit margin for each app to range from 6.5%-25%. The closer to 25% the better. Expenses for COGS are just what’s needed for running and operating the app. The gross profit should range from 65%-95%. All expenses in the ecosystem will be applied as expenses outside of COGS. They will be prorated if needed due to any shared infrastructure. Any R&D expenses before launch will also be included as expenses outside of COGS. All engineering, cybersecurity, AI tokens, and automation expenses (essentially any technology expenses) needed to run the platform post launch (or COGS) must amount to 35-5% of gross revenue
